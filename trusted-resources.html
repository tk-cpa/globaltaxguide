Trusted Resources

Every rate, rule, and treaty reference on this site is checked against the sources below before it is published. These are the anchors: official government and treaty portals first, then the Big Four and second-tier firm reference guides used as secondary, citable checkpoints.

Official primary sources

Government and treaty-body portals. These are authoritative - not summaries of the law, but the law and treaty text itself.

  • IRS - United States Income Tax Treaties, A to Z Primary

    Full text of every US income tax treaty and protocol, plus Technical Explanations where available.

  • IRS - Tax Treaty Tables Primary

    Summary tables of withholding rates by treaty article and income type, cross-referenced to Publication 901.

  • GOV.UK - Tax Treaties (HMRC) Primary

    The UK's full treaty network, related taxation documents, and multilateral agreements.

Big Four global tax guides

Free, practitioner-authored reference guides covering most of the world's tax systems. Used as secondary sources with a last-verified date noted on each country page.

  • PwC - Worldwide Tax Summaries Free

    Corporate and individual tax systems in 150+ territories, written by local PwC specialists and updated through the year.

  • EY - Worldwide Corporate Tax Guide Free

    Corporate income tax, treaty withholding rates, CFC and anti-avoidance rules across roughly 150 jurisdictions.

  • EY - Worldwide VAT, GST and Sales Tax Guide Free

    Indirect tax systems - scope, rates, registration thresholds, and digital economy rules - across 150+ jurisdictions.

  • EY - Tax and Law Guides Library Free

    Hub for EY's full guide set, including personal tax, transfer pricing, and estate and inheritance tax guides.

  • Deloitte International Tax Source (DITS) - Tax Highlights Free

    Country tax highlights for 130+ jurisdictions plus a rates database covering corporate, withholding, and VAT/GST rates.

  • KPMG Global - Tax Services Free

    Entry point to KPMG's global tax practice pages and member-firm Tax Rates Online tools for corporate, indirect, and individual rates.

  • KPMG - Corporate Tax Rates Table Free

    KPMG's online corporate tax rate comparison table, sourced from the OECD Data Explorer and updated regularly - useful primarily for OECD-member and major-economy rate cross-checks.

Second-tier firm resources

Additional practitioner guides used to cross-check specific topics, especially transfer pricing and Pillar Two implementation tracking.

  • Grant Thornton - Global Transfer Pricing Guide Free

    Country-by-country transfer pricing and Country-by-Country Reporting rules.

  • BDO - World Wide Tax News Free

    Rolling coverage of corporate and withholding tax rate changes across 160+ countries and territories, plus a dedicated Pillar Two implementation tracker.

  • BDO - Tax Publications ("Doing Business In..." country guides) Free

    Country-by-country tax rate, relief, and exemption summaries alongside broader business-setup guidance.

  • RSM - International Tax Planning Guide Free

    Annual guide covering cross-border structuring, Pillar One/Two (Amount B, GloBE) developments, and jurisdiction-specific international tax considerations.

This list will grow as we verify additional second-tier and boutique international guides. If a firm publishes a free, citable global or regional tax guide we should be tracking, we welcome the suggestion via cpavalidated.com.

Data and comparison resources

Independent, well-documented compilations used for cross-checking rate figures and tracking global minimum tax (Pillar Two) adoption status across jurisdictions.

  • Tax Foundation - Corporate Tax Rates Around the World Free

    Annual compilation covering 226 jurisdictions, sourced from OECD, PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, and Bloomberg Tax, with Pillar Two (QDMTT/IIR/UTPR) adoption status for every jurisdiction and a full downloadable dataset.

How we use these sources

Every figure on Global Tax Guide is checked against at least one source above, a national tax authority, or a primary statutory text in the jurisdiction's own official language before publication. Where English-language secondary compilations don't cover a jurisdiction, we go find the primary law directly rather than defaulting to a note that the rate couldn't be confirmed. Nothing on this site is generated by extrapolation or pattern-matching from other countries' rates.
