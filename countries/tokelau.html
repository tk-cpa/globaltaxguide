One-sentence summary Tokelau is a small dependent territory of New Zealand (three atolls, no airport, reachable only by boat) with a minimal, largely undeveloped conventional tax system - reflecting its tiny population (around 1,500) and subsistence/aid-dependent economy rather than a developed jurisdiction's tax code.

Tax System

Tokelau is a New Zealand territory (a non-self-governing territory under the UN framework, though New Zealand has supported Tokelau's self-determination efforts) with a very small population and limited formal economy; it does not operate a conventional developed-jurisdiction income tax system in the sources reviewed. New Zealand provides significant budgetary support to Tokelau given its very limited independent revenue base.
