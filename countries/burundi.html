One-sentence summary Burundi's corporate tax position: 30% standard rate (1% of turnover minimum if the company reports a loss). Personal income tax: Progressive: 0% up to BIF 1,800,000; 20% from BIF 1,800,001-3,600,000; 30% above BIF 3,600,000; flat 15% for non-residents. VAT/consumption tax: 18% standard VAT.
