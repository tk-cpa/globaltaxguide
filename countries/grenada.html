One-sentence summary Grenada's corporate tax position: Sources vary between 25% and 28% on net profits for resident and non-resident companies (non-residents taxed on Grenada-source profit only) - confirm the current rate directly with Grenada's Inland Revenue Division before relying on a specific figure. Personal income tax: Two brackets: 10% on the first EC$24,000 of taxable income, 28% on income above that threshold. VAT/consumption tax: 15% standard VAT.
