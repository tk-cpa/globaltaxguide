One-sentence summary Burkina Faso's corporate tax position: 27.5% standard rate; minimum tax of 0.5% of turnover applies; mining companies subject to separate Mining Code rates. Personal income tax: Progressive, 0% to 27.5% (Impot Unique sur les Traitements et Salaires for employment income). VAT/consumption tax: 18% standard VAT.
