One-sentence summary Saint Kitts and Nevis's corporate tax position: 33% standard rate on worldwide income for resident companies (non-residents taxed on Saint Kitts and Nevis-source income). Personal income tax: 0% - no personal income tax. VAT/consumption tax: 17% standard VAT - the highest standard rate in the Eastern Caribbean.
