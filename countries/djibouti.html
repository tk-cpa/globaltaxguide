One-sentence summary Djibouti's corporate tax position: 25% standard rate (Business Profit Tax); 3-year exemption for newly registered companies. Personal income tax: Progressive, reported ranges of roughly 2% to 30-33% depending on source. VAT/consumption tax: 10% standard VAT.
