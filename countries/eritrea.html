One-sentence summary Eritrea's corporate tax position: Reported at 30% (TaxAtlas) to 33% (other secondary sources) - confirm current rate directly given limited independent verification available for this jurisdiction. Personal income tax: Progressive, 2% to 30-33% depending on source; a separate 2% 'diaspora tax' applies to Eritrean nationals living abroad on worldwide income. VAT/consumption tax: No formal VAT; a sales tax applies at approximately 4% on goods and 10% on services.
