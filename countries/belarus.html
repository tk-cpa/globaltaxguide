One-sentence summary Belarus's corporate tax position: 20% standard (25% for banks/insurers and companies with taxable profit over BYN 25 million in a reporting period; preferential regimes in the Hi-Tech Park and Free Economic Zones). Personal income tax: Flat 13% for most personal income (16% for individual entrepreneur business income). VAT/consumption tax: 20% standard; reduced 10% rate for food, children's goods, and agricultural products; 0% for exports.
