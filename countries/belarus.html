One-sentence summary Corporate tax: 20% standard (25% for banks/insurers and companies with taxable profit over BYN 25 million in a reporting period; preferential regimes in the Hi-Tech Park and Free Economic Zones). Personal income tax: Flat 13% for most personal income (16% for individual entrepreneur business income). VAT/consumption tax: 20% standard; reduced 10% rate for food, children's goods, and agricultural products; 0% for exports.

Corporate Tax Rate

20% standard (25% for banks/insurers and companies with taxable profit over BYN 25 million in a reporting period; preferential regimes in the Hi-Tech Park and Free Economic Zones).

Personal Tax Rate

Flat 13% for most personal income (16% for individual entrepreneur business income).

VAT / GST Rate

20% standard; reduced 10% rate for food, children's goods, and agricultural products; 0% for exports.

Residency

An individual is a Belarusian tax resident if physically present in Belarus for more than 183 days in a calendar year, confirmed via TaxAtlas. Resident companies are those incorporated in Belarus. Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents are taxed on Belarus-source income.

CFC (Controlled Foreign Company) Rules: No

Belarus has no Controlled Foreign Company regime, confirmed directly via GSL's specialist tax-law profile: "there are no provisions regulating the procedure of taxation of controlled foreign companies" in Belarusian legislation.

Thin Capitalization

No statutory thin capitalization ratio was identified in available sources this session.

Treaty Network

Belarus has entered into 68 Double Tax Treaties, confirmed via GSL's named list, including with Austria, Azerbaijan, Armenia, Bahrain, Belgium, China, Cyprus, Germany, India, Iran, Israel, Japan, Kuwait, the Netherlands, Poland, Qatar, Russia, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, Syria, the UAE, the United States, and many others. Belarus has not signed the OECD's Multilateral Convention (MLI). GSL flags that treaty application with some Western countries has been affected by current international sanctions - the existence of a treaty on paper does not guarantee unaffected practical operation given the current sanctions environment. Belarus also participates in the Eurasian Economic Union, providing special tax and trade arrangements with Russia, Kazakhstan, Armenia, and Kyrgyzstan.

Sources: GSL - Belarus tax system and treaties (68 DTCs named, CFC confirmation), TaxAtlas - Belarus Tax Rates and System (2026), Lloyds Bank Trade Portal - Belarus Tax Rates.
