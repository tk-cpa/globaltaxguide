One-sentence summary Vanuatu's corporate tax position: 0% - no corporate income tax for local or international companies; International Companies can be exempt from most taxes for 20 years, paying a flat USD 300 annual fee instead. Personal income tax: 0% - no personal income tax, capital gains tax, inheritance tax, or wealth tax. VAT/consumption tax: 15% standard VAT (the principal source of government tax revenue); registration required above VUV 4 million annual turnover.
