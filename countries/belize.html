One-sentence summary Belize's corporate tax position: Most companies pay Business Tax on gross receipts rather than a conventional profits tax: 1.75% general trade, 6% professional services, up to 19% telecoms, with a 25% income tax applying instead to companies not subject to Business Tax (e.g. the oil sector). Personal income tax: Flat 25% on chargeable income above BZD 26,000-29,000 (threshold raised under 2025 amendments); territorial system - only Belize-source income is taxed. VAT/consumption tax: 12.5% General Sales Tax (GST); registration required above BZD 75,000 annual turnover.
