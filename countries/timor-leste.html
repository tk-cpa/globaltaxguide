One-sentence summary Timor-Leste's corporate tax position: 10% standard flat rate (30% for oil and gas contractors; 6% for oil and gas subcontractors); Supplemental Petroleum Tax also applies to oil and gas contractors. Personal income tax: Progressive 0% to 10% for residents on worldwide income; flat 10% for non-residents on Timor-Leste-source income (including Wage Income Tax withholding). VAT/consumption tax: No VAT; a limited sales tax applies at 2.5% on imported taxable goods and 0% on goods/services sold and delivered domestically.
