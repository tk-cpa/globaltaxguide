One-sentence summary Nepal's corporate tax position: 25% standard rate (30% for banks and financial institutions; 20% for special industries, exporters, and Special Economic Zone companies). Personal income tax: Progressive, 1% to 36%, with a 20% surcharge on income exceeding NPR 5 million. VAT/consumption tax: 13% standard VAT.
