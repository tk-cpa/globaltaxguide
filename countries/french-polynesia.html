One-sentence summary French Polynesia's corporate tax position: 25% standard rate (20% for renewable energy production; 33% for mining, financial institutions, and leasing companies, scheduled to decline gradually to 25% by 2027). Personal income tax: 0% - French Polynesia does not levy personal income tax on individuals, confirmed by International Labour Organization statistical methodology for the territory; also 0% wealth tax and 0% inheritance/gift tax between residents. VAT/consumption tax: 0% - no VAT or general sales tax; the territory relies on corporate tax and social contributions instead.
