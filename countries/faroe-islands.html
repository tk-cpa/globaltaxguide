One-sentence summary Faroe Islands's corporate tax position: 18% flat rate. Personal income tax: Progressive - national tax (from DKK 65,000/year) plus flat municipal tax (16-23% depending on municipality) plus church tax where applicable; non-residents/limited tax liability taxed at a flat 42%. VAT/consumption tax: 25% standard rate (matches Denmark's rate, though the Faroes set their own tax law independently under 1948 Home Rule).

Self-governing territory of the Kingdom of Denmark since 1948 (Home Rule), with its own tax legislation separate from Denmark; not an EU member and not part of the EU VAT area.
