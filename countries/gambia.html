One-sentence summary Gambia's corporate tax position: The higher of 27% of net profit, or 1% of turnover on audited accounts (2% on unaudited accounts). Personal income tax: The higher of scheduled progressive rates, or 1-3% of turnover depending on business size and audit status. VAT/consumption tax: 15% standard VAT.
