One-sentence summary Palau's corporate tax position: 12% Business Profit Tax (BPT) on net income for PGST-registered businesses (effective 1 January 2023, replacing the prior 4% Gross Revenue Tax); businesses under USD 50,000 annual gross income are exempt from GRT/BPT but pay a business license fee instead. Personal income tax: Progressive wage tax: 6% on the first USD 8,000 of gross wages, 12% above that, with no tax-free threshold. VAT/consumption tax: 10% Palau Goods and Services Tax (PGST).
