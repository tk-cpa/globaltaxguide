One-sentence summary Somalia's corporate tax position: Corporate Income Tax exists under Law No. 5 of 1966 (as administered by the Somalia Revenue Directorate/Federal Government of Somalia), but a current statutory percentage could not be independently verified from available sources this session - confirm directly with the Somalia Revenue Directorate. Personal income tax: Personal/payroll income tax (PAYE) applies under the same 1966 framework; a current statutory percentage could not be independently verified from available sources this session.
