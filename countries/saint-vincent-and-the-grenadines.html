One-sentence summary Saint Vincent and the Grenadines's corporate tax position: Broadly in line with regional Eastern Caribbean norms (confirm current corporate rate directly with the Inland Revenue Department; not independently verified this session). Personal income tax: Progressive rates on worldwide income for residents (non-residents taxed on local-source income only). VAT/consumption tax: 16% standard VAT (in effect since May 2017); 11% reduced rate for the hotel sector.
