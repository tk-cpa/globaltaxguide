One-sentence summary The Pitcairn Islands - the world's least populous national jurisdiction, with a population of just 57, Pitcairn has no income tax of any sort, for individuals or companies.

Tax System

"The Pitcairn Islands have no income taxes of any sort (for individuals and companies)." Pitcairn is a British Overseas Territory with the world's smallest permanent population of any jurisdiction (57 residents), accessible only by boat, with no airport or seaport.
