Political and Economic Status

Abkhazia is a partially recognized state on the eastern Black Sea coast, recognized as independent by Russia and a small number of other UN member states, while the United Nations and the majority of the world's governments consider it part of Georgia. Its economy runs on the Russian ruble and is substantially financed by Russian aid.
