One-sentence summary Transnistria (Pridnestrovie) is a breakaway strip of territory internationally recognized as part of Moldova, administered as a de facto state with support from Russia; no reputable source publishes verifiable standard corporate, personal, or VAT rates for it in English, so this page intentionally omits specific figures.

Political and Economic Status

Transnistria is a narrow strip of land on the eastern bank of the Dniester river, internationally recognized as part of the Republic of Moldova by the United Nations and virtually every UN member state. It has functioned as a self-governing, unrecognized state since 1990, with its own currency (the Transnistrian ruble), government, and armed forces, and receives substantial support from Russia. Moldova has been working to gradually harmonize customs, tax, and trade rules with the region.

Not independently verified this session. As of mid-2026, Moldova's Ministry of Finance has been developing mechanisms to apply Moldovan VAT and excise rules to specific categories of goods traded across the administrative line with Transnistria, which implies Transnistria's own indirect tax rules remain separate from Moldova's standard 20% VAT, but no Transnistria-specific rate was located.
