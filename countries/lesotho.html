One-sentence summary Lesotho's corporate tax position: 25% standard rate. Personal income tax: Top marginal rate 30%. VAT/consumption tax: 15% standard rate.

Corporate Tax Rate

25% standard rate.

Personal Tax Rate

Top marginal rate 30%.

VAT / GST Rate

15% standard rate.

Residency

Individuals file and remit personal income tax annually with Revenue Services Lesotho (RSL); specific day-count residency test details were not confirmed in available sources - businesses operating in Lesotho should confirm the precise individual residency criteria directly with RSL. Capital gains tax applies on disposal of immovable property or marketable securities, with the gain included in assessable income. Lesotho applies broad general anti-avoidance rules to transactions between associated persons.

CFC (Controlled Foreign Company) Rules: Not identified

No CFC provisions were located in available secondary sources on Lesotho's tax law. This is an honest gap rather than a confirmed absence - given Lesotho's close economic integration with South Africa (which does have detailed CFC rules) and its Southern African Customs Union membership, confirm the current position directly with Revenue Services Lesotho before relying on the absence of CFC rules for planning purposes.

Thin Capitalization

No thin capitalization or interest-deduction-limitation rules were located in available sources for Lesotho.

Foreign Bank Account / Foreign Financial Asset Reporting

No domestic Lesotho equivalent to the US FBAR/Form 8938 regime was identified in available sources.

Treaty Network

Lesotho has concluded double tax treaties with Botswana, Eswatini, Mauritius, and South Africa, per a 2025 practitioner guide. Note: a separate, authoritative UK government archive (HMRC/gov.uk) lists the 1997 UK-Lesotho Double Taxation Convention as "terminated" - this directly conflicts with the 2025 secondary source listing the UK as a current treaty partner. Given this genuine discrepancy in the sources, confirm the current UK-Lesotho treaty status directly with Revenue Services Lesotho or HMRC before relying on treaty relief for any UK-Lesotho cross-border matter.
