One-sentence summary Niger's corporate tax position: 30% flat rate (Impot sur les Societes). Personal income tax: Progressive, 1% to 35% monthly (Impot sur le Traitement et les Salaires). VAT/consumption tax: 19% standard rate (TVA, under WAEMU directive harmonization).
