One-sentence summary Cook Islands's corporate tax position: 20% standard rate for both resident (worldwide income) and non-resident (Cook Islands-source income) domestic companies. Personal income tax: Not independently confirmed from a reliable current rate-table source this session. VAT/consumption tax: Not independently confirmed this session (the Cook Islands does operate a VAT, locally called VAT, but the current standard rate was not verified from a primary source this session).

A self-governing state in free association with New Zealand. PwC's Worldwide Tax Summaries lists the Cook Islands (together with Niue) among New Zealand's associated 'privileged taxation jurisdictions.'
