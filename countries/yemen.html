One-sentence summary Yemen's corporate tax position: 20% flat standard rate (35% for telecoms, 50% for petroleum sector production-sharing income); civil conflict has significantly disrupted formal enforcement and split governance affects administration. Personal income tax: Progressive 10% to 15% (some sources cite up to 20%) under Income Tax Law 17/2010, with an annual exemption of YER 120,000. VAT/consumption tax: 5% General Sales Tax.
