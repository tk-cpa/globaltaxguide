One-sentence summary Afghanistan's corporate tax position: 20% flat rate under the Afghan Income Tax Law. Personal income tax: Progressive up to a 20% top rate (income under AFN 60,000 exempt); administration under the current Afghanistan Revenue Department, with limited independent verification of current enforcement practice. VAT/consumption tax: 10% VAT (introduced 2014-2015 with IMF support; base does not yet cover all goods and services).
