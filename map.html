Global Tax Guide is a free educational reference covering corporate, personal, and indirect tax rules for every country and territory in the world. Nothing on this site constitutes legal, tax, or professional advice, and no professional relationship is created by your use of it. Information should be verified against current primary authority in the relevant jurisdiction - statute, treaty text, and guidance from the local tax administration - before being relied on or acted upon. Each page shows a "last verified" date; tax law changes frequently, and changes since that date may not be reflected. Full disclaimer.
